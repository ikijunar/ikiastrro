### Strengths

- The strongest repeated pattern is **structured capability**: Saturn is exalted in D1, own sign in D3 and D16, strong in D27/D40, and the current Dasha repeats Saturn. This supports persistence, systems, and responsibility across learning, skills, and long-term work.
- **Knowledge and communication** recur: Mercury is the D1 ascendant lord, appears as Friend in D1/D3/D30, exalted in D45, and is tied to education or research in D24. The current Mercury Pratyantardasha makes this the most actionable short-term strength.
- **Protection through benefic support** appears in D9 Moon/Jupiter, D10 Sun/Venus, D11 exalted Jupiter, D20 Moon/Jupiter, and D60 exalted Jupiter. These are cross-chart supports for guidance, learning, public usefulness, and recovery after difficulty.
- Gains are not supported by D1 alone: D11 places Jupiter exalted in the 11th and Venus/Mercury in the 5th, giving the current model a meaningful secondary gains signal.

### Weaknesses

- Saturn's repeated D9/D10/D11/D20 vulnerabilities show that responsibility can carry **delay, isolation, career pressure, or restructuring**, even when D1 is strong.
- Moon-related stress signals recur in D1 8th, D5 8th, D12 8th, D24 1st with Enemy dignity, and D60 12th. The engine can flag emotional/privacy load, but it cannot diagnose health or psychological conditions.
- Mercury is strong in selected charts but weak or conflicted in D4, D7, D10, D24, and D60. Communication and education are therefore opportunities that require editing, verification, and patience rather than assumptions of effortless fluency.
- Venus is very strong in D1/D11/D12 but debilitated in D16 and conflicted in D24/D27. Material comfort, relationships, and creative output should be assessed by subject and chart instead of being generalised from D1 alone.

### Opportunities

- During Saturn–Saturn–Mercury, convert study and analysis into a durable output: a course, documented method, advisory service, portfolio, or repeatable workflow. D1, D3, D24, D45, and D10 all provide some support for this direction.
- Use D11 and D2 as separate checks for gains and wealth, then reconcile with D10 before making career or income claims. The current data favours steady accumulation over speculative timing.
- Use D4 and D16 for property, home, vehicles, and comforts; the mixed dignities favour maintenance, due diligence, and budget controls.
- Use D20 and D9 for private study, spiritual discipline, and meaning-making. The charts support practice and learning, but not a specific religious outcome without dedicated rules.

### Threats

- Treating every varga as equally predictive would create false certainty. D60 is birth-time sensitive; D6/D8/D30 contain risk symbolism that must not be converted into medical, accident, or death predictions without validated rules and external facts.
- The alternate D2-US must not be blended with the primary classical D2. Its placements are a reconciliation view, not an extra vote in the wealth result.
- Many divisional charts currently lack displayed nakshatra names/padas and do not have automated subject-level confirmation scores. Cross-chart SWOT statements are therefore qualitative evidence summaries, not calibrated probabilities.
- The current system does not yet combine transits, Ashtakavarga, annual charts, or dated life events. The all-chart view can rank themes, but it cannot safely time a specific event.
