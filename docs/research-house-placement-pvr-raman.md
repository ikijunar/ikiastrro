# House-placement rules: PVR and B. V. Raman comparison

**Status:** Comparison across houses 1–12, informed by both volumes; full Volume II OCR complete. Research note, not an implemented or exhaustively normalised rule catalogue.

The two sources agree substantially on house meanings and on judging placements through
lordship, strength, aspects, occupants and appropriate divisional charts. They are also
complementary: `SRC_PVR_INTEGRATED` explicitly refers readers to `SRC_RAMAN_HTJH` for
detailed house results. That referral does **not** establish agreement with every individual
placement prediction. Their treatment of house boundaries has a documented difference.

## Adopted convention — 2026-09-07

The user confirmed **whole-sign houses for ikiastrro**, following `SRC_PVR_INTEGRATED`.
Each sign is one house, counted inclusively from the selected reference sign; Lagna is
the default reference. Apply this consistently to D1 and the relevant divisional chart.

Use `SRC_RAMAN_HTJH` for supplementary placement interpretations while retaining its
conditions and provenance. Record whole-sign application as a project adaptation where
necessary. Rules requiring Bhava cusps, Bhava-sandhi or a different house assignment remain
research-only unless separately reformulated and reviewed for whole-sign use. Do not
silently translate cusp-dependent results into whole-sign rules.

For the saved Ramakrishnan sample (Aries Lagna), Rahu in Cancer is in house 4 and Ketu in
Capricorn is in house 10. The illustrative Sripati assignments discussed during comparison
are not ikiastrro placements. This decision confirms the existing house geometry; it does
not change planetary longitudes, sign dignity, or which source originally supplied a rule.

## Scope and evidence

“House placements” covers two separate rule families:

- **Planet in house:** for example, Sun occupying the third house.
- **House lord in house:** for example, the fifth lord occupying the second house.

House meanings, reference points, house systems and modifying conditions are prerequisites
for both. This note compares traditional textual claims; agreement is not empirical validation.

Sources and passage locators are in the [source registry](research/reference-sources.md#house-placement-comparison-evidence-2026-09-07).
The local primary-text extracts were read directly. The reviewed `SRC_RAMAN_HTJH` material now includes the existing Volume I extract and the
new full Volume II OCR. Detailed placement sections for houses 7–12 were located and
reviewed, with key claims checked against scans. [Extraction coverage and quality](research-raman-volume2-extraction.md)
records the 482-page result and its limits. Full-book extraction does not mean every
case-study chart or every individual rule has been verified.

## Actual difference: how a planet acquires its house number

| Topic | `SRC_PVR_INTEGRATED` | `SRC_RAMAN_HTJH` | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| House boundaries | PVR-H3 explicitly recommends one sign per house, counted from the selected reference, and rejects the described equal-house and Sripathi/Bhava-chakra alternatives. | RAM-H2 explicitly distinguishes Bhava from Rasi, prioritises Bhava in house delineation, and asks for Rasi, Bhava and Navamsa consideration. | **Difference.** Store the house system with each placement. These Raman passages do not supply enough arithmetic to implement a particular cusp method. |
| Transferring a detailed result | The project uses the PVR whole-sign baseline. | A Raman rule may depend on a Bhava placement that differs from the Rasi placement. | Importing the prose under whole-sign counting is a project adaptation unless equivalence is checked; it is not demonstrated author agreement. |

Illustration only: if a planet is in the second sign from Lagna but falls in the first
house under a chosen cusp-based calculation, the two placements address different rule
conditions. Do not merge their outputs as if both authors assessed the same placement.

## Important qualifications exposed by Volume II

- **Bhava boundaries affect actual judgment.** RAM-IIB's profession example discounts the
  Moon's tenth-house influence because it is at Bhava-sandhi. This is direct evidence that
  the house-system difference has practical consequences, not just different terminology.
- **References already overlap.** RAM-IIK defines Karakamsa through the Atmakaraka's
  Navamsa sign and uses it in occupation/spiritual passages. Raman's timing sections also
  use house lords counted from the Moon. Neither should be labelled PVR-only.
- **Qualitative terms are not ready-made thresholds.** “Strong”, “beneficially disposed”,
  “afflicted”, “associated” and “in strength” need explicit interpretation before execution.
- **Avoid false karaka equivalence.** RAM-IIK's house-karaka list includes Saturn for the
  sixth. PVR-H2's planetary-reference table includes Mercury for the sixth. The tables have
  different uses; do not overwrite one with the other or label either a universal karaka map.
- **The source itself can be inconsistent.** RAM-II9's afflicted ninth-lord-in-fourth passage
  says there may be no domestic unhappiness, immediately followed by misery and parental
  disharmony. The scan confirms this wording: it is not merely an OCR correction to make.
  Mark that outcome unresolved until another edition or erratum clarifies it.
- **The authors' caveats belong to the rules.** RAM-II10 explicitly describes occupational
  indications as tendencies rather than fixed professions; RAM-IIK acknowledges that the
  post-death state cannot be verified. Preserve those limits when summarising the text.

## Rule capture requirements

For the next extraction pass, each candidate needs:

- Stable rule ID and family, planet or owned house, and occupied house.
- Chart, reference point and house-system scope; unknown scope remains explicit.
- Topic and narrowly paraphrased result, with favourable/adverse branches separated.
- Required conditions, exceptions and unresolved terms such as “well disposed”. Do not
  invent a numeric threshold for a qualitative phrase.
- One registered `SourceRefCode` per source assertion, plus an evidence key resolving to
  the registry; cross-source comparison stored separately from the assertion.
- Comparison status, counterpart evidence if found, and review status. Text located is
  different from complete transcription, executable rule, or verified implementation.

Keep the established PVR-first baseline. Add Raman detail with its provenance and any
house-system adaptation visible. Do not combine source names into a new synthetic authority,
or count a PVR referral and its referred Raman statement as independent corroboration.

The next implementation preparation is a **first-house pilot**: normalise first lord in
houses 1–12 plus planets occupying the first house, with all qualifications. Both volumes
are now available for repeating this across all houses. A complete 144 lord-placement or
108 nine-graha-placement catalogue remains a coverage target, not a count of completed
executable rules. One placement can produce several conditional, topic-specific rules.
